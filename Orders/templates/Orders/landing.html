Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What is TrainPOS?

TrainPOS is train food order management software for railway and eCatering restaurants, combining automatic order capture, live train ETA, billing and sales reports in one dashboard.

Who is TrainPOS built for?

It is built for restaurants that receive food orders for passengers travelling by train.

Can TrainPOS manage orders from multiple railway food platforms?

TrainPOS brings supported railway food-order provider emails into one operational dashboard.

How does TrainPOS receive eCatering orders?

After a restaurant connects its order Gmail, supported order emails can be captured and organized into TrainPOS orders.

Does TrainPOS show live train arrival information?

When train data is available, TrainPOS can show expected arrival, delay and arriving-in information for the delivery station.

Can I track COD and prepaid railway food orders separately?

Yes. Reports distinguish COD and prepaid/online value alongside other sales information.

Does TrainPOS print bills?

Yes. TrainPOS includes compact thermal bill printing with order, passenger, payment and item details.

How much does TrainPOS cost?

TrainPOS costs ₹5,999 per year, effectively ₹500 per month.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New restaurants receive a 15-day free trial with no card required to begin.

Do I need special hardware?

No complicated restaurant hardware is required to start. Thermal bill printing works with a compatible printer and browser setup.
